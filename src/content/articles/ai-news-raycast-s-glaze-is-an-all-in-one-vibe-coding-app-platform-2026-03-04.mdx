### What is Raycast's Glaze?

Raycast is a launcher app designed to make your computer experience smoother by streamlining tasks and providing quick access to various applications and tools. Recently, they introduced Glaze, a platform that takes things a step further by offering what are called **vibe codes**. But what exactly are vibe codes? 

- **Vibe Codes**: Think of them as snippets or pieces of code that others have created, which you can use to perform specific functions or tasks on your computer or applications. It’s like having a toolkit where you can grab pre-built solutions without having to create everything from scratch.
- **Glaze Store**: This is a directory filled with these vibe codes. When you visit the Glaze Store, you can explore various codes created by others, which expands your possibilities significantly.
