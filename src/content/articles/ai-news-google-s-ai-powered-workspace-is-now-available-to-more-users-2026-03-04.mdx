Google is expanding its AI features to make your online experience smoother and more productive! Let’s dive into what this means and how it could change the way you manage your day-to-day tasks.

## What Is Google’s AI-Powered Canvas?

Google has recently announced that its AI-powered workspace feature called **Canvas** is now available to more users across the U.S. Initially available in its Gemini app, Canvas allows users to create documents, plan projects, and even write code—all in real-time. Now, this feature is being integrated into Google’s AI Mode in Search, which means it is not only accessible to more people but also comes with enhanced functionalities.

### A Closer Look at Canvas

Think of Canvas as a virtual workspace that lives right within Google Search. When you're working on a project or plan, Canvas allows you to:

- Organize your ideas intuitively
- Collaborate more effectively on projects
- Draft and edit documents on the fly
- Access the latest information directly from Google’s search results 

You can use Canvas alongside your chat, making it easy to write out plans or create tools without switching between different apps.
