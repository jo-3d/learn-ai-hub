## Understanding Anthropic's New AI Features: Claude Code and Cowork

### What’s New?

Recently, Anthropic, a leading AI research company, has introduced exciting updates to its AI assistant, Claude. These updates have made Claude even more useful by allowing it to perform tasks directly on your computer without needing to set anything up. Imagine having a virtual assistant that can open files, browse the web, and even run development tools—all while you’re away from your desk!

When you activate this feature, Claude will ask for your permission before taking any action, making it a safe and user-friendly option. This means you can trust Claude to handle tasks efficiently while you're busy or unavailable.
