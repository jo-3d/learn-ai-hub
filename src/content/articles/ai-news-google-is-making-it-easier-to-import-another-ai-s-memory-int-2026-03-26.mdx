Google has just introduced some exciting new tools for its AI chatbot, Gemini, that make it a lot easier for you to switch from one AI to another without the need to start from scratch. Let's break down what this means and how it can benefit you!

## What Are the New Features?

On Thursday, Google unveiled two features called "Import Memory" and "Import Chat History." These tools allow Gemini to take in what your previous AI already knows about you, so you won’t have to retrain it with all the information again. This is a game-changer if you've developed a relationship with your current AI and don't want to lose that connection when trying out a new one.

### How Does It Work?

1. **Import Memory**: This feature lets you bring over your existing AI’s memory. Imagine having a conversation with your current AI where it remembers your preferences, interests, and past interactions. With "Import Memory," you can easily transfer all of that knowledge into Gemini.

2. **Import Chat History**: This tool enables the migration of your chat history. If you have had enlightening exchanges with your current AI, you can import those conversations into Gemini, helping it understand you better right from the start.

To use these features, here’s a simple process:

- Copy a suggested prompt provided by Gemini and paste it into your old AI.
- The old AI will then give you an output, which you can copy.
- Next, paste that output into Gemini, and voilà! Your new AI now knows all those important details.
